# 🧠 HRM: A Comprehensive Tutorial on Hierarchical Reasoning Models 🧠

![GitHub last commit](https://img.shields.io/github/last-commit/google/generative-ai-docs)
![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/google/generative-ai-docs?style=social)

Welcome to this in-depth tutorial on **Hierarchical Reasoning Models (HRM)**. This repository is your ultimate guide to understanding, building, and applying HRMs to solve some of the most complex problems in AI today. 🚀

Whether you're a student, a seasoned researcher, or a developer looking to expand your AI toolkit, this tutorial will guide you through the core concepts, hands-on implementations, and advanced applications of hierarchical reasoning.

## ✨ Key Features

*   **Comprehensive Coverage**: From the fundamentals to advanced topics, we've got you covered.
*   **Chapter-by-Chapter Structure**: Easy-to-follow, organized chapters to guide your learning journey.
*   **Practical Examples**: Learn by doing with practical examples and implementations.
*   **Case Studies**: Explore real-world applications of HRMs in NLP and robotics.
*   **Future-Focused**: Get a glimpse into the future of hierarchical reasoning and its potential.

## 🤔 Who Is This For?

*   **Students**: Get a solid foundation in a cutting-edge area of AI.
*   **Researchers**: Deepen your understanding of hierarchical models and their theoretical underpinnings.
*   **Developers**: Learn how to implement and apply HRMs in your own projects.
*   **AI Enthusiasts**: Anyone curious about the next wave of AI and reasoning models.
